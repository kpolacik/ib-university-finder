      Frequently asked questions

      What is this site, and can I apply through it?
      A free tool that helps IB students find bachelor’s and other first degrees (like medicine) in 11 European countries, by IB points, subjects, deadlines and cost. You can’t apply here: each programme links to its official page and says where to apply.
      Can I rely on what it says?
      Use it to build a shortlist, then check everything on the official page before you apply. The data was collected automatically from official pages, and we’re checking it over time; a programme someone has checked says “Checked by …”.
      What should I check before I apply?
      The deadline and any earlier dates in “Deadline notes”, the IB subjects and grades, your maths course (AA or AI), the English requirement and any entrance exam. Some of these are on university-wide pages or the national application site, not the programme page.
      How do my IB points work here?
      With My IB points set, programmes whose points you can’t reach are hidden (for Irish courses, even counting the HL Maths bonus). The rest say whether they’re within your points, only with HL Maths 4+, or can’t be compared. Being within the points doesn’t guarantee a place: subjects, language and selection count too.
      Why do so many programmes show no IB points?
      About half have no IB points figure, including every one in Germany, Austria and Italy. Where we know why, the row says so (“Entrance exam”, “Test in cutoff”); otherwise no figure has been found. These programmes always stay in your results.
      What do “Not published” and “Not checked yet” mean?
      “Not published” means we haven’t found a figure, date or rule. “Not checked yet” means nobody has looked, never that there’s no requirement. Neither hides a programme: filters keep unknown values and say so on the row.
      How are Irish CAO points turned into IB points?
      Most Irish courses show last year’s CAO cutoff (≈), converted with the CAO’s IB table; cutoffs move every year. HL Maths at grade 4+ adds 25 CAO points, and a cutoff from 601 to 625 can only be reached with it. UCD shows its own IB minimum; HPAT Medicine shows the CAO minimum (480 points, 35 IB), then ranks places with the HPAT.
      Which fee is shown?
      The yearly fee for EU students. In the UK that’s the international fee, converted from pounds; EU students pay it since 2021, with some exceptions. In Ireland it’s the Free Fees student contribution plus levy, not the higher EU fee some pages print.
      What does the yearly cost include?
      The fee plus 12 months of estimated living costs. Where a row says “national minimum”, that’s the least a visa needs and real costs are usually higher. It doesn’t add one-off costs like deposits or travel. Set “My living costs” to use your own figure.
      How do I change my Setup answers?
      Press “Edit my answers” next to the programme count (in Filters on a phone). Your answers are just filters, so you can also change them directly.
      What’s the difference between My list and Compare, and what’s stored?
      My list keeps what you save: group it, export it or share it by link. Compare puts 2 to 4 programmes side by side and is separate. Both, your filters and your language stay only in this browser; no account, no tracking.
      Who makes this, and how do I report a mistake?
      Jakub and Leon, IB students. The countries are the ones students at our school asked about most, and more are coming; the data is updated every few weeks. Found a mistake? Tell us at ibuniversityfinder@gmail.com.
